# Evaluation notes

[evaluation.md](evaluation.md) is the **blind** held-out result: the first and only run of the engine on the frozen held-out set, generated by script and committed unedited. Read it first. This page records what happened around it.

## Order of events (13 Oct 2026)

1. **The generator was written and both sets were frozen** before any engine tuning: tuning seed 20261013, held-out seed 16102026, eight personas each. `backend/synth/HELDOUT.sha256` pins the held-out file, and a test fails if it changes.
2. **Two generator bugs were fixed and the held-out set was refrozen**, still before any engine tuning and without looking at held-out results.
   - A planted drift of +18 on a senior whose normal is 122 never crossed 140. That isn't a hand-over case under the brief's own rule, so drifts now land at 152 or above.
   - Overlapping events overwrote each other's check-in texts.
3. **The engine was tuned on the tuning set only.** Changes:
   - A family simulated to respond the day after a handover.
   - A 14-day cooldown on repeat handovers of the same kind.
   - A steps baseline that excludes the last 14 days.
   - The AAC weekly cap applied to handovers only.
   - A stricter mild-drift note.
4. **Blind held-out run**: red flags 5 of 5, real problems 16 of 17, no false handovers. Against the fixed rule, CareLoop handed over drifts 3 to 10 days later.
5. **The one miss was a rule-ordering bug.** An already-handed-over BP drift kept matching first, was held back to a digest note, and stopped the table, so three "not feeling well, dizzy" days that followed (h04, 29 Mar) were never looked at. It is fixed in `ladder.py`: a cooling-down kind is skipped and the table looks for any other problem. There is a regression test, `test_a_new_problem_is_not_hidden_behind_an_old_handover`.
6. **Re-run after the fix (NOT blind)**: 17 of 17 real problems and 5 of 5 red flags, with no false handovers. This number must not be presented as a held-out result.

## For the pitch

- Lead with the losses: the fixed "BP > 140" rule spots drifts sooner, by about 2 to 4 days on average.
- What CareLoop buys for that:
  - It catches what the fixed rule cannot see: danger symptoms said in passing, missed medicine, days of feeling unwell, isolation.
  - It sends no false handovers, against about 1.3 to 1.8 per persona-month for the fixed rule.
- Everything is synthetic, and the thresholds are placeholders until a clinician signs off.
