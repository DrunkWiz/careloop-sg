CareLoop SG

Quiet, AI-assisted support for seniors with high blood pressure, between doctor visits

The problem

Most of hypertension care happens at home, between visits. Readings drift, pills get missed, a senior feels unwell and says nothing. Families worry from afar, and seniors who live alone may have no one checking at all. Alert apps make it worse: a fixed "BP over 140" alarm fires so often that families stop listening.

What CareLoop does

A short daily check-in, by voice or big buttons, in English, Singlish or Mandarin. CareLoop learns each senior's own normal and stays quiet while things are normal for them. When something changes, it steps up gently: it asks, then nudges, then notes it, and only hands over to the family (or to their Active Ageing Centre if they have no family) when a problem persists, with the evidence. Red flags (a dangerous reading or a danger symptom) reach the family at once, every time.

Who uses it

  • Seniors: a large-text, low-effort app; it explains what each medicine is for and which vaccines or check-ups are due.
  • Family: a quiet app with the trends, the evidence when needed, a shared care plan, and a one-page GP brief to print.
  • Centre staff: a caseload console for seniors who live alone, sorted by who needs a call first.

Key features

  • Personal baselines and context (rain, haze, festivals) before anyone is alerted
  • Handovers that wait for a person to choose a step; CareLoop never calls anyone itself
  • Consent: the senior chooses what family sees
  • Readings from a Fitbit or OMRON cuff via Apple Health / Health Connect (simulated in the demo)
  • Medicine notes in plain words; an unknown medicine goes to the pharmacist, never guessed

How it uses AI, safely

Rules decide; the AI only reads and writes words. A large language model reads each check-in to catch what simple rules miss (in a live test, "my chest feel like got elephant sitting on top" became a chest-pain red flag that keyword rules alone had missed), and words the gentler messages in the senior's own language and tone. It never chooses who is told or whether a number is dangerous. Every message passes a guardrail (no diagnosis, no dose changes, no invented numbers) or a fixed template goes out; red flags always use fixed wording. With no model at all, CareLoop still works on rules and templates.

Tested blind

On a frozen, held-out set of synthetic seniors (720 days), compared with the usual "alert above 140" rule:

5 of 5red flags caught (fixed rule: 3 of 5)
16 of 17real problems handed over
0false alarms per senior-month (fixed rule: 1.29)

The honest trade-off: a slow blood-pressure drift is handed over about 3 days later than the fixed rule. In return, families get no false alarms, and CareLoop sees what one number can't: missed medicine, days unwell, isolation.

How it's built

React web app (installable on a phone) · Python FastAPI backend · a rule engine with one thresholds file · a language layer that is the only path to the model (Tencent Hunyuan or any OpenAI-compatible model) · open data from data.gov.sg (haze, rainfall, centre locations) · 183 offline tests.
